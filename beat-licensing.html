Non-exclusive licences

A lease generally allows more than one artist to license the same beat. The final agreement will specify permitted releases, streams, performances, videos, monetisation, credits and any limits.

Trackouts

A trackout licence includes separate audio stems for greater mix control. It does not automatically transfer beat ownership or exclusive rights.

Exclusive enquiries

Exclusive availability and price must be confirmed directly. Previous non-exclusive licences may continue, depending on the final agreement.

Samples and credits

Any required producer credit, sample restriction or clearance responsibility will be stated in the licence. Do not release a song until you have received and reviewed the applicable written licence.
