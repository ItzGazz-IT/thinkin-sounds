Booking terms.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

An enquiry is not a confirmed booking. The studio will confirm the service, date, price and deposit in writing.

Session length and price

Recording has a one-hour minimum and sessions may run for up to eight hours. Prices marked “from” depend on the project scope. Any extra time or work must be agreed before it is charged.

Deposit

A 25% deposit secures a slot after availability and scope are confirmed. The studio will explain the payment due and how a change or cancellation affects the deposit before payment.

Rescheduling and cancellation

Contact the studio as early as possible if plans change. The applicable rescheduling, cancellation and refund arrangement will be the one confirmed in writing for your booking.

Files and permissions

You are responsible for supplying usable files and for having permission to record, adapt and release any beat, sample, vocal or other material you provide.

Revisions and delivery

Two revisions are included unless a quote says otherwise. The standard maximum turnaround is 48 hours after all required files, references and direction are received. New creative direction, replacement recordings or additional deliverables may require a revised quote.

Approval

Review files carefully before approval or release. Final delivery and usage rights depend on full payment and any written project terms agreed with the studio.
