    Good to know

    Questions before
    the session.

    Can I bring my own beat?

    Yes. Bring the highest-quality file available and make sure you have permission to record and release music over it.

    What should I send for mixing?

    Send each track from the same start point, plus your rough mix and one or two reference songs. We’ll confirm the preferred file format before work starts.

    Can I book mastering without mixing?

    Yes. Send an approved stereo WAV with enough headroom and no limiter unless the studio asks for a reference-limited version too.

    Does submitting the form confirm my session?

    No. It opens a prepared WhatsApp message. Your booking is confirmed only after the studio agrees the date, scope, price and deposit.

    How do revisions and turnaround work?

    Standalone work normally takes up to 48 hours once we have the required files and direction. For project bundles, we agree the delivery date upfront and include two revision rounds per song. Larger edits or new creative direction may need a separate quote.
